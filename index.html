Your work order number is [#] — please keep that handy for reference. I'm sorry this hasn't been resolved yet.

SOP Documentation
Escalation Handling & Case Logging Process

Based on the inbound call workflow I followed at RealPage — acting on behalf of property management to receive, document, and escalate resident concerns to the appropriate management team.

Process Overview

1. Greet caller per approved script · 2. Verify identity via account number or unit address · 3. Pull up resident account and review any existing cases in CRM · 4. Listen to concern and document it accurately with the appropriate category tag and priority level · 5. Advise resident that their concern has been logged and will be escalated to the property management team · 6. Create or update the case in CRM with full call notes · 7. Route escalation to the property management team for resolution — follow-up is handled on their end.
